🌱 About FoodShare

FoodShare is a food donation platform designed to reduce food wastage and connect people with available food donations.

Our Purpose

Every day, a large amount of food can be wasted while many people may need food. FoodShare provides a simple way for users to donate extra food instead of throwing it away.


How It Works

1️⃣ Donate

Users can add information about available food.

2️⃣ Find Food

Other users can view available food donations.

3️⃣ Collect

A user can collect an available food donation.

4️⃣ Reduce Waste

Donating extra food helps reduce unnecessary food wastage.
